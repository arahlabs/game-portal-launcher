# Game Portal Launcher

[![Game Portal Launcher: all your games in one home](assets/feature-graphic.jpg)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.arahlabs.gameportal)

**A console-style home screen for your Android games.** Game Portal Launcher puts your games front and center as cover cards, works with a game controller as well as touch, and keeps your other apps one tab away.

### [▶ Get it on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.arahlabs.gameportal)

This repository is the public place to **report bugs** and **request features**. The app's source code is not published here.

[Report a bug](../../issues/new?labels=bug) · [Request a feature](../../issues/new?labels=enhancement) · [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.arahlabs.gameportal) · [Privacy Policy](https://gameportal.arahlabs.com/privacy-policy)

## Features

### Your games come first
- **Home is for games.** Your pinned games come first, then the games you played most recently, with the active game's artwork filling the screen behind them. Apps that are not games live in **All apps**.
- **All games, Favorites and your own categories**, each as a tab on the top strip. Give a category its own icon.
- **Pin to Home**, favorites, hide apps, sort A–Z or by recently played, and **Random game** when you can't decide.
- **Show as:** if an app is listed in the wrong place, mark it as a game, an emulator or not a game.
- **Search** across all your games and apps.

### Six home layouts
**Sunset, Spotlight, Dock, Shelf, Hub and Glass**: each has its own look and its own way of presenting your games. Every layout works in light and dark mode and in portrait and landscape. In landscape, game tabs can show as a single row or a grid.

### Made for controllers
- Fully playable with a **game controller or keyboard**, as well as touch.
- Fixed, easy-to-learn buttons: L1/R1 switch tabs, START opens Settings, SELECT opens the menu, L3 picks a random game. A built-in **button guide** shows them all.
- An **on-screen keyboard** you can type on with a controller.
- Fast scrolling with the right stick, controller battery levels, and optional navigation sounds.

### Emulated games
- Add your **game folders** (*Settings › Library › Game folders*). The launcher uses Android's folder picker, so it needs no storage permission.
- Emulated games appear next to your installed games on Home, in All games and in search, and also have their own **Emulation** tab. Your emulator apps are listed in an **Emulators** tab.
- When you start an emulated game, you choose which installed emulator to use, with an option to remember that choice for the game. If no suitable emulator is installed, the launcher suggests ones on Google Play.
- The launcher does **not** include or download any games. Use only game files you are legally allowed to use.

### Make it yours
- **Your own artwork:** import a picture for any game, then move and zoom it, with touch or a controller.
- **Game card sizes:** choose how many cards fit across and down each screen, in portrait and landscape.
- Theme (system, light, dark or night), animation speed, full screen mode and more.
- **Big screens:** scales up for tablets, and for a monitor or TV connected to your phone over HDMI or USB-C.

### Backup and restore
Save your setup (settings, favorites, categories, hidden apps, artwork and emulator choices) to a file, and restore it later or on another device. Before anything is restored, you see a preview of every change.

### Languages
English, Bahasa Indonesia, Español, Português (Brasil), Français, Deutsch, 日本語, 한국어, 简体中文 and Русский.

### Requirements
Android 8.0 or later. [Download from Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.arahlabs.gameportal).

## Reporting a bug

Before opening an issue, please [search the existing issues](../../issues?q=is%3Aissue) to see if it has already been reported. If it has, add a 👍 or a comment with your details instead of opening a new one.

A good bug report includes:

| | |
|---|---|
| **App version** | Android *Settings › Apps › Game Portal Launcher*: the version is at the bottom |
| **Device** | e.g. *Samsung Galaxy A54* |
| **Android version** | e.g. *Android 16* |
| **Layout** | Sunset, Spotlight, Dock, Shelf, Hub or Glass |
| **Input** | Touch, keyboard, or the controller model |
| **Orientation** | Portrait or landscape (and whether an external display is connected) |
| **Steps to reproduce** | What you did, step by step |
| **What happened / what you expected** | Include a screenshot or a short screen recording if you can |

For problems starting an emulated game, please also include the **emulator app and its version** and the **game file's extension** (for example `.iso` or `.zip`).

## Requesting a feature

Open an issue that describes **what you would like to do** and **why**. The problem you want solved helps more than a specific solution. One idea per issue, please: it keeps the discussion focused.

### Currently not planned
To save you time, these are outside the launcher's scope for now:
- Android TV devices and TV boxes (phones and tablets connected to a TV *are* supported)
- Mouse control
- Emulators that are not available on Google Play
- Downloading games or game files (ROMs)

## Please don't post

- **Personal information:** email addresses, account details, or full backup files (a backup lists the games you have).
- **Game files, or links to them.** Issues that ask for or share copyrighted game files will be closed.
- **Security vulnerabilities.** Please don't open a public issue. Report them privately through this repository's *Security* tab.

## Privacy

Game Portal Launcher has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your library, games, files and settings stay on your device. The only data that leaves it is a license check confirming your copy came from Google Play. See the [Privacy Policy](https://gameportal.arahlabs.com/privacy-policy) for details.
